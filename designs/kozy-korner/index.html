Meet Keneisha

Kozy Korner is Keneisha Mitchell's licensed child-care home in North Houston. She has been licensed by the State of Texas since November 2022 and runs the daycare herself, out of her own home.

She keeps the group small, with room for up to six children, and keeps long hours, 6 AM to 7 PM, Monday through Saturday, so parents with early shifts, late shifts and Saturday shifts have a place they can count on.
