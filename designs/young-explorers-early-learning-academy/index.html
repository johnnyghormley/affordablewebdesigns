Meet the director

Sheila Johnson Wilson

Owner & Director, Young Explorers Early Learning Academy

Sheila runs Young Explorers out of her home in Pearland, where she has operated a Texas-registered child-care home since 2018. She cares for children from infancy through school age, and keeps her doors open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so working parents never have to choose between their shift and their child.

Her goal is simple: a nurturing place where kids build academic readiness, friendships, confidence and creativity.
