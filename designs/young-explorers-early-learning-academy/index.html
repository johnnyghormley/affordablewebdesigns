★ Meet the director

Sheila Johnson Wilson

Owner & Director, Young Explorers Early Learning Academy

Sheila has been caring for Pearland children since 2018, when she registered Young Explorers with the State of Texas. She cares for infants, toddlers and school-age kids, and keeps her doors open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so working parents never have to choose between their shift and their child.

Her goal is simple: a nurturing place where kids build academic readiness, friendships, confidence and creativity.
