About the owner

Meet Latisia Villareal

Little Hands Day Care is Latisia's licensed child-care home in Northwest Houston. She has been licensed by the State of Texas since September 2017 and keeps the group small, up to 12 children, in a warm, home-style setting.

Latisia cares for infants, toddlers, pre-K and school-age kids, with meals and snacks provided. She opens at 6:00 in the morning and stays open until 7:00 at night, Monday through Friday, so working parents don't have to rush. Give her a call to set up a visit and see the space for yourself.
